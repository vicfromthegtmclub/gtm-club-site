Messaging / Members tool

Say it in their words.

One row per buyer pain, mapped to the proof and the exact line that earns a reply. Fill a row, get it checked against the rules that kill most cold outreach, then copy the whole matrix into your Sheet. Nothing to download. Nothing to sign up for.

01Who feels it

Segment, not industry. Add a size band and one qualifier.

The title that feels the pain, not the one who signs.

02Why now

Something observable this quarter, with a time window. If you cannot detect it, it is not a trigger.

03What hurts

Lift it from a call, a review or a support ticket. If it sounds like your website, rewrite it.

A number, a headcount, or a deadline. Vague cost means a weak row.

04What you say

The outcome after you. No feature names, no adjectives.

Name, number, date, source. No proof means the row is a hypothesis.

Opens on the trigger or the pain, carries the proof, ends on a question that is easy to answer.

One observation, one question. No pitch.

05What they push back with

The real one you hear, not the polite one.

A question that reframes. Never a pitch.

Validated means you heard this on a call.
